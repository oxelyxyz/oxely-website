Clear, start to finish.

How the Oxely App is designed to work once it launches. Read the full flow →

01

Connect

Join the waitlist, then connect your own wallet when the app opens. Each vault shows who can use it and its deposit cap.

02

Allocate

Pick a vault and read its strategy, limits and risks on one page. Funds can only go where the written rules allow.

03

Follow & withdraw

Track positions on-chain and read plain-language updates. Withdraw by redeeming shares under the vault's published terms.

Complex inside. Simple outside.

Many markets, one vault

Behind each vault, positions may sit across several venues. You hold one share and follow one value.

HARD LIMIT

Limits that don't move

Every vault has written caps per venue, set before launch. If a position reaches its limit, it stops there.

Custody

Your wallet, your shares

Vault shares sit in your own wallet, not in an Oxely account.

Rules

Limits written first

Every vault publishes its strategy and limits before it takes a deposit.

Visibility

Open by default

Positions are on-chain, so anyone can check them, any time.

Access

No middlemen

Connect a wallet and use it yourself, within each vault's terms.

Safety comes before returns.

We would rather launch later than launch unsafe. These are the rules we hold ourselves to.

Independent audits before launch

No vault opens to deposits before its contracts are reviewed by outside auditors.

Self-custody

You keep your keys. Oxely can't move funds out of your wallet.

Limits and monitoring

Each vault has hard limits per venue and is watched around the clock.
