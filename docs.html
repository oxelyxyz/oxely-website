Access, deposits and withdrawals

How the Oxely App is designed to work once it launches, from connecting a wallet to taking your money out.

Who can use Oxely

Access will open to the waitlist first. Some vaults may only be available in certain countries or to certain types of users. Each vault page will show who it is open to before you can deposit.

The deposit flow

01ConnectConnect your own wallet. Oxely never holds your keys.
02ReadRead the vault's strategy, limits, risks and terms.
03DepositApprove and deposit the vault's asset, up to its cap.
04Receive sharesVault shares arrive in your wallet.

Withdrawals

You withdraw by returning (redeeming) your vault shares. You receive the vault's asset based on the share value at that time, minus any fees.

  • Each vault publishes its withdrawal terms before launch: how often you can withdraw and how long it takes.
  • Vaults keep part of their funds in reserve so normal withdrawals can be paid quickly.
  • In unusual market conditions, withdrawals may take longer. Any such rules will be written in the vault terms up front.

Fees

Fees will be shown on every vault page before you deposit. Oxely does not plan any hidden or deposit-time fees that are not listed there.

Never send funds to any address claiming to be Oxely. During the preview, Oxely does not accept deposits of any kind.

Vaults

Oxely Stable

A stablecoin vault for people who want their dollars to stay dollars, while earning lending interest.

StatusIn development
Deposit assetsUSDC, USDT (planned)
Risk profileConservative
Target returnPublished at launch
WithdrawalsTerms published at launch

How it plans to earn

Deposits are lent out across several on-chain lending markets. Borrowers pay interest, and that interest is what the vault earns. Returns go up and down with lending demand.

Limits

  • A maximum share of the vault in any single lending market.
  • A minimum reserve kept aside for withdrawals.
  • Only stablecoins and markets on an approved list.

The exact numbers and the approved list will be published before launch.

Main risks

Smart contract risk in the vault or the lending markets, a stablecoin losing its peg, and lending rates falling. See Risks and controls.

Oxely Hedge

A market-neutral vault that aims to earn from funding payments and price differences, while keeping overall price exposure close to zero.

StatusIn research
Deposit assetUSDC (planned)
Risk profileModerate
Target returnPublished at launch
ReportingOn-chain + weekly notes

How it plans to earn

The vault holds a long position and a short position of similar size in the same asset. If the price rises or falls, one side gains while the other loses, so they roughly cancel out. The vault aims to earn from the funding payments between those positions.

What "market-neutral" does not mean

It does not mean risk-free. Funding can turn negative, a trading venue can fail, and fast price moves can cause losses before a hedge is adjusted.

Main risks

Negative funding, venue or counterparty risk, imperfect hedges, and smart contract risk. See Risks and controls.

Share value and transparency

How the value of a vault share is worked out, and what you can check for yourself.

Share value

When you deposit, you receive shares. Each share is a slice of everything the vault holds.

share value = (vault assets − liabilities − fees owed) ÷ shares outstanding

If the vault earns, share value goes up. If it loses, share value goes down. Your number of shares stays the same.

What you can check on-chain

  • Your share balance, in your own wallet.
  • Deposits and withdrawals into the vault.
  • The vault's on-chain positions and where they sit.

What we will report

Each vault will publish regular plain-language updates explaining what changed and why. Any part of a strategy that cannot be seen on-chain will be clearly labelled as reported by Oxely.

Every vault can lose money. This page lists the main risks and the controls designed to reduce them.

Controls reduce risk; they do not remove it. Only deposit what you can afford to lose.

FAQ

Short answers to common questions. Can't find yours? Ask us.

Is Oxely live?
Not yet. Oxely is in development and does not accept deposits. Join the waitlist to hear when the first vault opens.
Does Oxely hold my funds?
No. You keep your keys and your vault shares sit in your own wallet. Funds move only when you sign a transaction.
What returns can I expect?
Target returns will be published with each vault's full strategy at launch. Returns are never guaranteed and can be negative.
Can I withdraw any time?
Each vault will publish its withdrawal terms before launch. Most are designed for regular withdrawals, but timing can be longer in unusual market conditions.
Have the contracts been audited?
Not yet. No vault will open to deposits before its contracts are reviewed by independent auditors, and reports will be published.
Someone from "Oxely" asked me to send funds. Is it real?
No. Oxely does not accept deposits during the preview and will never ask you to send funds to an address by message. Only use the official site, oxely.xyz.
